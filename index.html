Reji James
Founder & Director · Carsbuddy
"I didn't build a detailing studio. I built a standard — and then took it to five cities."

Reji James is a serial automotive entrepreneur based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. His career spans two decades across luxury car sales, electric mobility, and premium automotive care.

As Founder & Director of Carsbuddy Pvt Ltd (est. 2020), he built South India's most trusted car detailing and paint protection brand from the ground up — expanding to five cities and partnering with global names like XPEL and Modesta Ceramic.

Prior to Carsbuddy, Reji led high-value sales operations at Tafe Access and established an authorized dealership for River Mobility electric vehicles in Mysore — consistently proving his ability to spot, enter, and scale in high-growth automotive segments.
